01The Full Weekend

Four Days of Celebration

The wedding is one day — the celebration is a whole weekend. Here’s how it unfolds.

Thursday · Dec 9

Henna / Mehndi at Home

An intimate evening of henna for the bride and close family — Cayla, Sheri, Stephanie, Sangeetha, Seetha, and maybe a few more hands.

Friday · Dec 10

Haldi, Lunch & the Welcome Party

10am Haldi puja at home (family + a few friends), lunch at noon, and from 2pm friends decorate Briscoe Manor. Henna for anyone who wants it at 4pm (two artists), then the Welcome Party 6–8pm at the Holiday Inn in Sugar Land — an open-house with beer, wine, heavy appetizers, and maybe live music.

Saturday · Dec 11

The Wedding Day

Indian ceremony pictures first, the Barat, the puja, an Indian vegetarian lunch, chai in the courtyard with games and the photo booth, the Western ceremony outside with bubbles, cocktail hour, then reception — first dance, cake, toasts, father-daughter and mother-groom dances, and dancing into the night.

Sunday · Dec 12

Farewell Breakfast

9am at the hotel for guests staying over — a last warm hello before everyone heads home.

06Good to Know

FAQ

Everything our guests ask us most — answered in one place.

What should I wear? Is there a dress code?

The dress code is festive Indian or formal — think cocktail dresses, suits, or traditional Indian attire. Colours are very welcome; the palette of the day is warm, rich, and celebratory.

Are kids welcome?

Absolutely — children are part of the celebration. There will be lawn games and flexible seating during the lunch block, and the reception is family-friendly.

Where are the ceremony and reception?

Everything happens at one venue — ceremony, lunch, chai, and reception. Full address and directions are in the Travel section above.

Is there parking?

Yes — free on-site parking, plus valet for the evening reception. See Travel for details.

Will there be food? What about dietary needs?

A full Indian buffet is served during the protected lunch block. Vegetarian options are plentiful, and we’ll do our best to accommodate allergies — please note them on your RSVP.

Can I bring a plus-one?

We’d love to celebrate with everyone you love — please include your party size on the RSVP so we can plan seating and food.

What will the weather be like?

December in Richmond, TX is mild — highs near 65°F, cooler by evening. The chai hour is outdoors, so bring a light layer if you run cool.

Where should I stay?

We’ve reserved room blocks at the Holiday Inn Houston SW – Sugar Land Area and the Holiday Inn Express & Suites Stafford NW – Sugar Land — details and booking links in the Accommodations section.

Are there gifts / a registry?

Your presence is the only present we need. If you’d like to contribute to our future together, a small contribution toward our [honeymoon fund / home fund] would mean the world.

Can I take photos and share them?

Please do! Use our hashtag [#Hashtag] and leave your favourite shots in the Digital Guestbook so we can relive the day through your eyes.
