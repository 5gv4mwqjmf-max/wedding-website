Welcome

Namaste, and welcome

One day that weaves Indian tradition with modern elegance — a Saturday in December: the Barat, the puja, lunch, chai, a Western ceremony, and dancing into the night. We can’t wait to celebrate with you.

See the Day

Chapter One

Morning — The Barat

Wedding-day pictures first, then the Barat: the groom’s family and friends bring Keshava to the wedding hall with music and dancing, thirty minutes before the ceremony begins.

Morning

Chapter Two

The Indian Puja

The couple is seated as their families surround them. Sai briefly explains each step of the ceremony, the bride and groom thank their guests, and everyone throws rice as the couple leaves.

Two Hours

Chapter Three

Lunch & Chai

A relaxed Indian vegetarian lunch, then chai in the courtyard — lawn games, a photo booth, a digital guest book, and time to simply be together.

Afternoon

Chapter Four

The Western Ceremony & Reception

An outdoor Western ceremony with bubbles as the couple departs, a cocktail hour, then dinner, toasts, the first dance, and dancing under the lights.

Evening
